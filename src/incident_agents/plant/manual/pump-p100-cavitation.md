# P-100 series centrifugal pump: cavitation

Symptom: noisy operation, fluctuating discharge pressure and a drop in flow, often with broadband vibration.

Cause: suction pressure below the required NPSH, for example a clogged suction strainer or a low tank level.

Action: clean the suction strainer STR-80 and verify the tank level. No spare parts usually required. Downtime 1 hour.
