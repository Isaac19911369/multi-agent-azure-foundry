# P-100 series centrifugal pump: bearing over-temperature

Normal drive-end bearing temperature is 55-65 °C at nominal load.

- Above 80 °C: inspect lubrication; check grease quantity and the condition of the seal SEAL-220.
- Above 90 °C: stop the pump; risk of bearing seizure.

A gradual temperature rise combined with increasing vibration indicates bearing wear (see high vibration).
