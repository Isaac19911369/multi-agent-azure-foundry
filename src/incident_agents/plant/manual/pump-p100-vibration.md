# P-100 series centrifugal pump: high vibration

Symptom: vibration_rms above 4.5 mm/s (ISO 10816 zone C) or a steady upward trend of more than 0.5 mm/s per day.

Most likely causes, in order of frequency:
1. Drive-end bearing wear. Usually accompanied by a bearing temperature rise of 8 K or more above baseline. Replace bearing kit BRG-6310 and re-grease. Planned downtime 4 hours.
2. Impeller imbalance from deposits. Vibration rises without a temperature change; flow drops slightly. Clean the impeller. Downtime 3 hours.
3. Misalignment after maintenance. Vibration jumps suddenly after a service; check coupling alignment with the laser tool. Downtime 2 hours.

Safety: above 7.1 mm/s (zone D) the pump must be stopped within 24 hours to avoid shaft damage.
